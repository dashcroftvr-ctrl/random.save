Gaz & Serg

What can I do with them? Walk up to them — they turn, look at you and say hello. Get too close and they'll back off.
Poke them with a fingertip (or a controller) — chest or head. Keep poking and they lose patience.
High-five — hold your hand up near one of them for a moment and he'll raise his to meet it. Slap it!
Ignore them for a while and they get bored — Serg might nod off.
Floating panel to your left (press with a finger, or point and pull the trigger): dance-off, Gaz's gag, bring them in front of you, sound.
On this screen: click a character to poke, drag to look around, scroll or W/S to walk closer, hold H to hold a hand up for a high-five.
